## Diagram: engine attack paths, containment as nesting, scale and accessibility (WP-4-4)

No golden recording changes: the SVG diagram is not part of the golden files.

### Changed

- The diagram draws `report.attack_paths` and computes none of its own.
  The old search ran over an undirected view of the graph, so a shared VPC joined unrelated subnets and invented routes.
  Each path is drawn hop by hop in the direction of its edges, with arrows and numbered markers in the colour of the path severity.
  A marker sits only on the connector that points along a hop, never on one that runs the other way.
  When the report has no paths the section says "No attack paths found" instead of disappearing.
  Until the engine computes paths (WP-4-1), a real scan shows that note.
- Containment is drawn as labelled nested frames (a VPC around its subnets, a subnet around its instances, a namespace around its workloads, a Dockerfile stage around its instructions) and no longer as an arrow from every child to its parent.
  A container with members is a frame, not a box, and its findings show on the frame badge and tooltip.
  A container with no members stays an ordinary node.
- Arrowheads have a fixed size, and ports on a node side spread over most of its width, so the arrows of a busy node no longer stack on one vertex.
- A Dockerfile stage with more than 6 instructions is one node.
  The findings of its instructions move to the stage badge and tooltip, and the edges between stages (`FROM <stage>`, `COPY --from`) are drawn.
  `format_diagram(..., expand_docker=True)` draws every instruction.
- Label placement uses a uniform-grid index over obstacles and route segments, builds the avoid list once, and tries at most 64 positions per label.
  A generated 300-node plan whose every connector crosses a trust boundary renders in under a second, where it took over 20 seconds.
- Above 150 nodes the diagram collapses nodes by zone and type, shows a notice banner and logs a warning.
  `format_diagram(..., lod_threshold=None)` keeps every node.
- Findings table cells wrap onto up to three lines instead of being cut at a fixed width, so the target of "Trust boundary crossing: internet -> private" stays visible.
  Only an overflowing last line ends in an ellipsis, and the row tooltip has the full text.
  The table shows at most 200 rows (`max_rows`), followed by an overflow row that points to the JSON and SARIF report.
- The table, summary bar and node badges use the effective severity when the engine set one.
- Attack path captions use at least 9 px text, wrap instead of cutting at 18 characters, and the blocks are spaced apart.
- The header timestamp has minute resolution and is left out when the report has none, so two renders of the same input with `--no-timestamp` are byte-identical.
- The root `svg` has `role="img"`, a `title`, a `desc` and `aria-labelledby`.
  Every id in `defs` carries a per-document prefix derived from the input file name, and ids of elements built from resource addresses are sanitized and unique.
- The font stack is `Inter, system-ui, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- A Secrets Manager secret or key node is drawn as a data store, and an API node like a process.
- A Dockerfile instruction node is named `run.3` rather than `3`.

### Added

- `format_diagram` takes `max_rows`, `expand_docker` and `lod_threshold` as keyword arguments.
- `SpatialIndex` in `formatters/diagram_routing.py`, and `place_on_route` accepts indexes as well as lists.
